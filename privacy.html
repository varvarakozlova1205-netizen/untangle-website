Privacy Policy / Datenschutzerklärung

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version

Berlin Bureaucracy Navigator is a static information tool. It has no accounts, no login, no database, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The answers you give in the questionnaire and the steps you tick off are handled only inside your browser while the page is open. They are never sent to a server, and they're gone when you reload or close the page.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted by [HOSTING PROVIDER]. When you open a page, the host's servers automatically process technical data so they can deliver the site and keep it secure. This includes:

  • your IP address
  • the date and time of the request
  • the page or file requested
  • the referring page, if your browser sends it
  • your browser type and operating system

This is necessary to serve the site and to detect and prevent misuse. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in running the site securely and reliably). Logs are kept only as long as needed for these purposes and then deleted. This data isn't combined with other sources or used to identify you.

Fonts

The Roboto font is hosted on this site's own server, and Georgia is already installed on your device. No data is sent to Google Fonts or any other third-party font provider.

Links to other websites

The checklist links to official websites such as service.berlin.de and the Berlin immigration office. Nothing is sent to those sites until you click a link. Once you do, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you (Art. 15)
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17)
  • restrict how your data is processed (Art. 18)
  • receive your data in a portable format (data portability, Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
  • withdraw consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). This site doesn't currently ask for consent for anything.

To use any of these rights, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. Because the only personal data involved is the host's short-lived server logs, there may be little or nothing to hand over, but you'll always get an answer.
